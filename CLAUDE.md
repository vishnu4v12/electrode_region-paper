# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code in this repository: the J. Phys. D manuscript on the near-electrode layers of an atmospheric-pressure argon arc. `README.md` has the layout, the validation-figure pipeline and the build command. Read it first.

## Companion repository: the solver

The solver is **PlasmeX**, in `../PlasmeX` (separate git repo, with its own `CLAUDE.md`: build, run, decks, the Newton integrator, verification rules). Read that file before rerunning a case or interpreting a plotfile.
- Nothing from PlasmeX is copied here. `figures/validation_fig/plasmex.py` imports `exe/bohm.py` from the live tree (`$PLASMEX_ROOT`, default `~/work/PlasmeX`).
- **Canonical runs (user, 11 Oct 2026):** the base cases below and the sweeps in `../PlasmeX/runs/base_sweep` are the paper's results unless the user corrects them. Ask before rerunning or replacing any of them.
- **Results section** (`sections/results.tex`): the base cases run in `../PlasmeX/runs/base_case_anode` and `../PlasmeX/runs/base_case_cathode`, each with its deck in the run directory. Figures: `python figures/results_fig/base_case.py <anode plt> <cathode plt>`. It also prints the numbers for table `tab:base`. The `% Run record` comment in `results.tex` names the plotfiles used.
- **Results sweeps** (`sec:res-sweep`): `python figures/results_fig/base_sweep.py` reads the last steady plotfile (a case's `tight/` if present) of every case in `../PlasmeX/runs/base_sweep` plus the two base cases. It writes `base_sweep_T`, `base_sweep_J` (rows: q_w, j_i/j, phi_s/phi(L), sheath width x_s; the T_e(0) and T_wall rows were dropped 12 Oct) and `base_sweep.csv`, and prints the steady check of each run (`base_sweep_report.txt`). The cathode J sweep ends at 1.5e8, the user's choice: no steady state was reached above it.
- **Momentum balance and collisionality** (`sec:res-momentum`, 12 Oct 2026): `python figures/results_fig/base_sheath.py <anode plt> <cathode plt>` writes `base_momentum` (every term of eqs mom-e/mom-i plus the neglected species inertia, a posteriori) and `base_sheath` (mean free paths, Boltzmann relation, u_i/u_B), and the numbers in the text (`base_sheath_report.txt`). Its term decomposition mirrors `../PlasmeX/src/aider.H`; the retained terms must sum to ~0 (it prints the check).
- **Relaxation times** (`sec:res-time`, 11 Oct 2026; rewritten 12 Oct): `python figures/results_fig/base_timescales.py` reads the +2 % current-step records in `../PlasmeX/runs/base_timescales` (its `README.md`: procedure, eps checks, what failed) through that directory's `analyse.py`, and writes `base_timescales.{pdf,png,csv}` and the numbers quoted in the text (`base_timescales_report.txt`). User's choice (12 Oct): axial acoustics removed (the real arc is 3-D), so the cathode uses the heavy_inertia 1e-4 records and the anode the physical-sound ones (identical there); t_99 vs J and T_bulk is the headline; tau is quoted once in the text (the timescales table was removed 12 Oct, and the section no longer names the time integrator); no domain-length results in the paper.
- **Validation section** (`sections/validation.tex`): the decks are `../PlasmeX/exe/input_files/almeida1e7_inputs` and `semenov1.5e7`. The plotfile behind each figure is kept next to its `compare.py`.

## Figure conventions

- Distance from the electrode on a log axis. **Cathode figures have the cathode on the LEFT; anode figures have the anode on the RIGHT** (the anode axis is mirrored). This applies to both the results and the validation figures.
- **Results figures** (`base_case.py`) use a log-linear distance axis, u = ln x + x/X_C with X_C = 50 µm (`matplotlib` function scale, inverse by Lambert W). It is logarithmic near the electrode (sheath visible) and smoothly linear towards the arc end, with no joint. A piecewise log|linear axis was tried and rejected: even with a C¹ joint the curvature jump showed. Ticks are in µm. The validation figures keep their plain log axis.
- Building: something in the editor (probably the LaTeX extension's build-on-save) deletes `main.aux/.log/.pdf` when it overlaps a terminal build. If citations come out undefined or `main.bbl` is empty, rerun the full `pdflatex -> bibtex -> pdflatex x2` chain.
- Two-case figures put the **cathode in the left column/panel and the anode in the right**. The panel letters follow, so the cathode is (a), (c), ... in the text.
- The electrode edge of every profile panel is marked by `figures/validation_fig/electrode_mark.py` (`mark_electrode`): a hatched strip behind the curves, a thick frame line, and a label on the top panel. Panel tags and curve labels go on the side away from it.
- The electron energy budget plots the divergence of the electron energy flux as one term, `-div h_e` (bohm's `NDIVH`), not split into its enthalpy, conduction and thermal-diffusion parts.
- **Sheath edge** (12 Oct 2026): `base_case.sheath_edge` is the one definition, the 1 % crossing of |n_i - n_e|/n_i log-linearly interpolated between cells (the cells there are 15 nm / 61 nm, too coarse for the cell centre). Table tab:base, the sweeps and base_sheath.py all use it.
- **Ion impact energy** (12 Oct 2026): the local 3/2 k T_eff estimate is gone from the paper. Only the cathode MAXIMUM is given, in sec:res-heat: the Wannier mean energy at the sheath edge plus e phi_s (1.5 + 4.1 = 5.6 eV, <= 38 MW/m^2), justified by the sheath's 1-2 ion collisions. `base_case.py` prints it.
