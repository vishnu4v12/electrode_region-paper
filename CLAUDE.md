# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code in this repository: the J. Phys. D manuscript on the near-electrode layers of an atmospheric-pressure argon arc. `README.md` has the layout, the validation-figure pipeline and the build command. Read it first.

## Figure conventions

- Distance from the electrode on a log axis. **Cathode figures have the cathode on the LEFT; anode figures have the anode on the RIGHT** (the anode axis is mirrored). This applies to both the results and the validation figures.
- **Results figures** (`base_case.py`) use a log-linear distance axis, u = ln x + x/X_C with X_C = 50 µm (`matplotlib` function scale, inverse by Lambert W). It is logarithmic near the electrode (sheath visible) and smoothly linear towards the arc end, with no joint. A piecewise log|linear axis was tried and rejected: even with a C¹ joint the curvature jump showed. Ticks are in µm. The validation figures keep their plain log axis.
- Building: something in the editor (probably the LaTeX extension's build-on-save) deletes `main.aux/.log/.pdf` when it overlaps a terminal build. If citations come out undefined or `main.bbl` is empty, rerun the full `pdflatex -> bibtex -> pdflatex x2` chain.
- Two-case figures put the **cathode in the left column/panel and the anode in the right**. The panel letters follow, so the cathode is (a), (c), ... in the text.
- The electrode edge of every profile panel is marked by `figures/validation_fig/electrode_mark.py` (`mark_electrode`): a hatched strip behind the curves, a thick frame line, and a label on the top panel. Panel tags and curve labels go on the side away from it.
- The electron energy budget plots the divergence of the electron energy flux as one term, `-div h_e` (bohm's `NDIVH`), not split into its enthalpy, conduction and thermal-diffusion parts.
- **Sheath edge** (12 Oct 2026): `base_case.sheath_edge` is the one definition, the 1 % crossing of |n_i - n_e|/n_i log-linearly interpolated between cells (the cells there are 15 nm / 61 nm, too coarse for the cell centre). Table tab:base, the sweeps and base_sheath.py all use it.
- **Ion impact energy** (12 Oct 2026): the local 3/2 k T_eff estimate is gone from the paper. Only the cathode MAXIMUM is given, in sec:res-heat: the Wannier mean energy at the sheath edge plus e phi_s (1.5 + 4.1 = 5.6 eV, <= 38 MW/m^2), justified by the sheath's 1-2 ion collisions. `base_case.py` prints it.
