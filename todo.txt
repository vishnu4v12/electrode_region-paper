PRIORITY 0 — must be fixed before ANY quantitative sheath number is quoted
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Put the dielectric relaxation time tau_diel = eps0/sigma_e into the
   adaptive dt limiter (ComputeDt, src/PlasmeX.cpp). Currently computed and
   printed but never applied; the shipped CFL under-resolves it, and the
   resulting sheath drop is a factor 2.4 too small (0.715 V vs a converged
   1.702 V), with wall n_e 2x high and wall E 2x low. This is not a
   modelling improvement, it is the difference between the code solving its
   own discretized equations correctly or not. Cost: ~10x in wall time; it
   WILL move both regression baselines in PlasmeX/CLAUDE.md and needs a
   deliberate, recorded rebaseline.
2. Once (1) is fixed, redo the mesh/timestep convergence study with
   Delta z and Delta t varied INDEPENDENTLY (not both tied to a single CFL
   number), and report both convergence curves explicitly in Results. A
   study that only varies max_level at fixed CFL is not a valid mesh
   convergence test (Delta t moves with it) and must not be reported as one.
3. Report the collisionless ion-impact-energy bound, e*(phi_edge - phi_wall),
   as a diagnostic alongside the Wannier T_eff-based impact energy at every
   sheath-resolved run. Currently the local closure gives (3/2)kT_eff =
   4.92 eV, which EXCEEDS the entire 1.703 V sheath fall — a impossible
   result, not merely an inaccurate one. Do not publish an ion-impact-energy
   or electrode-heat-flux number until this is resolved (see item 6 below).

PRIORITY 1 — needed to write section 3 (Results) at all
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Bulk-arc LTE validation run, independent of the electrode/sheath work:
   reproduce a free-burning-arc case comparable to Hsu/Etemadi/Pfender 1983
   and Hsu & Pfender 1983 (200 A, ~10 mm gap, argon) away from the
   electrodes, and compare predicted centerline/radial temperature profile
   and column voltage drop against their reported T_max ~21,200 K and
   phi_D ~13.3 V (1T) / their 2T companion values. This establishes that the
   LTE bulk closure (Section 2) is trustworthy before layering the
   near-electrode model on top of it.
5. Anode/cathode-fall benchmark run: with the dt fix (item 1) in place,
   report the predicted electrode fall voltage and its sign, and compare
   directly against the three-way convergent negative-anode-fall result:
     - Dinulescu & Pfender 1980 (theory):      -1.5 to -2.7 V
     - Sanders & Pfender 1984 (experiment):    -2.1 to -1.4 V
     - Liang & Trelles (3D non-eq CFD):        up to -4.2 V
   This is the single most citable, falsifiable validation target available
   and should be the centerpiece of Results. Note PlasmeX's current 1-D
   test problem is set up as an electron-repelling (negative-fall-like)
   sheath already — confirm the sign convention and geometry (ARC_SGN,
   which electrode is modelled) map onto anode vs cathode consistently
   before comparing numbers.
6. Fix or explicitly caveat the ion impact energy before reporting any
   heat-transfer number. Candidates, in order of effort (see PlasmeX
   CLAUDE.md "Open work" and doc/sheath/sheath_validity.tex ranked fix
   list): (a) cap/report against the collisionless bound (item 3, cheap,
   diagnostic only); (b) revisit whether the two-temperature Wannier
   closure is being evaluated at a self-consistent T_eff given the
   actually-resolved field profile, since the failure mode found is not
   "a bit off" but "exceeds the energy budget," which usually indicates a
   closure being evaluated well outside its derivation regime rather than
   a small calibration error.
7. Add the missing elastic electron-neutral energy-loss term,
   n_e * delta * nu_en * (3/2) k (T_e - T_gas), to the electron energy
   equation before extending the simulation domain or relaxing the
   Dirichlet T_e inflow boundary condition. At wall conditions this omitted
   term is ~23x the retained Joule heating term; it currently doesn't
   corrupt the reported solution only because the energy-relaxation length
   is ~4.5x the current domain size. Any larger-domain or longer-time
   result MUST include this term first, or the reported T_e will be
   systematically wrong (biased away from T_gas, i.e. away from LTE).
8. Produce the per-quantity validity table for the FINAL (post-item-1-7)
   model, not the diagnostic-run table recorded during development
   (reproduced in Model/Methods sections from
   PlasmeX/doc/sheath/sheath_validity.tex). Confirm which entries change
   once the dt limiter and elastic loss term are fixed — several verdicts
   (e.g. "electron flux inside sheath: not usable") were diagnosed
   specifically against the unconverged/incomplete model and may improve.

    that Benilov 2016 found insensitive to sheath-model choice. This is the

    reproduces those robust integral results AND gives trustworthy local
    quantities (species fluxes, ion impact energy) where the
    matching/homogenization approaches do not.
11. Sensitivity study: quantify how much the predicted sheath voltage drop,
    species fluxes and ion impact energy change if the electron wall flux
    is switched from the current field-independent 1/4 n_e cbar form to a
    Hagelaar-type field-aware form (matching what is already done for
    ions). This directly tests whether the "electrode is over-determined
    under current-driven operation" limitation noted in PlasmeX/CLAUDE.md
    is a first-order or second-order effect on the reported results.
12. Evaluate the accuracy cost of NOT having Scharfetter-Gummel
    discretization (removed from the tree; the current scheme is
    operator-split upwind-drift + implicit-diffusion). Quantify the excess
    numerical diffusivity D_num = D_e * psi / 2 (psi = cell Peclet number,

    physical D_e, and state explicitly in Results whether this is
    negligible or needs reinstating Scharfetter-Gummel before the interior
    (not just wall) sheath flux can be trusted.
